Use this entry only after the original source's actual owned writers and leader are gone and its exact latest complete joint has an actual `AB_JOINT_FULL_SHA_CPU_PASS` proof. It refuses an older audited checkpoint when a newer joint exists. It consumes the prior full-file-SHA and CPU-state evidence using its unchanged file seals rather than repeating those large reads. An actual restore must still be verified on the GPUs and followed through five new complete outers and the next real start.

Unsaved optimizer writes, prefixes and rollout fragments are not imported or stitched into the complete trajectory.
